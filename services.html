How We Work

Engineering services, structured like a project.

Every engagement runs against the same four-stage discipline — from initial brief through to documented sign-off.

01 / Brief

Specify the work.

Drawing, line list or RFP submitted via our trade desk. An engineer reviews within one working day.

02 / Quote

Documented response.

Written quote with availability, lead time, materials, and any substitution notes — flagged in plain language.

03 / Deliver

Despatched or mobilised.

Stocked lines couriered the same day. Fabrication and on-site work scheduled against your maintenance window.

04 / Sign-Off

Paperwork on the desk.

Mill certificates, weld reports, test data and OEM documentation supplied with the final invoice.
