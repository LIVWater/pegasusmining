The Founding Story

From one bay in Boksburg to a national supply network.

Pegasus was founded in 2013 to fix a problem the founders saw on site every week: critical components held up by suppliers who couldn't read a drawing, couldn't trace a part, and couldn't despatch when it mattered. Twelve years later, we still trade on those three commitments.

What We Stand For

Reliable supply. Documented quality. Engineering judgement.

Our customers don't have time for stock-outs, substitutions they didn't ask for, or paperwork that arrives a week late. Pegasus is built to deliver against engineering specification — every line, every time.

Our Commitments

Three principles we trade on.

01 / Traceability

Documented supply chain.

Mill certificates, OEM serial records and country-of-origin documentation supplied on request — supporting your SHEQ and audit obligations on every order.

02 / Technical Depth

Engineers on the desk.

Quotes are prepared by people who can read a drawing. Specification cross-references and OEM-equivalent substitutions are validated before they land in your inbox.

03 / Logistics

Nationwide despatch.

Daily despatch from Boksburg to the mining belts in Limpopo, Mpumalanga, the Free State and the Northern Cape. Critical lines couriered same-day.
